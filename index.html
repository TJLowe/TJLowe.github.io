HOUSE

HOUSE (Home Operations, Utilities and Services Environment) is a personal home automation app. It is used only by its owner, Tom Lowe, and is not available to anyone else.

Privacy policy

HOUSE connects to its owner's own Google account to read and label his email and, later, to suggest calendar entries.

  • Data is processed only on the owner's own home server.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • No data from any other person's account is accessed.
  • Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google account's security settings.
